Field-driven topology

PolyCarve

Drive a carve through geometry from an arbitrary scalar attribute or field.

Instead of reducing the problem to a simple primitive parameter, PolyCarve is intended for procedural cuts and reveals driven by data already living on the geometry. That makes complex field-based effects a direct operation rather than a custom topology exercise.

Surface-aware transfer

Geodesic Operations

Follow the surface when Euclidean distance gives the wrong answer.

For transfer, falloff, propagation, and analysis on folded or nearby surfaces, straight-line distance can jump across gaps that are close in space but far apart along the mesh. Geodesic operators are aimed at the topology-aware version of those problems.

FAQ

Before you buy.

Is Operators a node pack?

No. The product is positioned as a specialized computational toolkit. Convenience matters, but the focus is on operations that add meaningful capability or substantially improve difficult technical workflows.

Does Workflow come with Operators?

No. Workflow and Operators are separate standalone products. Pragmatic Suite combines both.

Which Houdini versions and operating systems are supported?

The final shipping compatibility matrix will be published before checkout is enabled.

How do company seats work?

Company licenses use a floating concurrent-seat pool. A company purchasing for even one employee starts at the 1–5 tier.
